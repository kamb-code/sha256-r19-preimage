# Reduced-Round SHA-256 Compression Preimages

This repository contains two separate research manuscripts, their source,
forward verifiers, computed witnesses, experimental records, and supporting
analysis.

## Manuscripts

### Original 19-round construction

**A Practical Preimage Attack on the 19-Round SHA-256 Compression Function
via a Global sigma0-Difference Table**

- PDF: [`paper_r19_final.pdf`](paper_r19_final.pdf)
- Source: [`paper_r19_final.tex`](paper_r19_final.tex)
- Filing record: [`RESUBMISSION_R19.md`](RESUBMISSION_R19.md)

This paper presents the original random-context construction and seven
forward-verified witnesses. Its 240-attempt campaign was stratified: the
screened arm produced 3 events in 120 attempts and the control arm produced 0
in 120. The pooled 3/240 value describes that campaign; it is not an estimate
for uniformly sampled contexts. The newer manuscript below supersedes its
performance figures while leaving its algebraic construction and archived
measurements intact.

### Context-shaped 19- and 20-round construction

**Context Shaping for 19- and 20-Round SHA-256 Compression Preimages**

- PDF: [`paper_submask_r20.pdf`](paper_submask_r20.pdf)
- Source: [`paper_submask_r20.tex`](paper_submask_r20.tex)
- Filing record: [`SUBMISSION.md`](SUBMISSION.md)

This paper chooses context words satisfying `a4 = a5` and derived conditions
`e8 = e9 = 0xFFFFFFFF`. The resulting 19-round system is triangular and its
remaining equality is the exact bitwise condition
`Maj(a4,a3,a2) = a3`. Recorded 19-round experiments agree with the stated
lookup-distribution model. The often-quoted 12 ms figure is a derived marginal
CPU estimate after a reusable 16-GiB table has been built, not an observed
cold-start latency.

At 20 rounds one further exact schedule equality remains. Tests of its
residual are consistent with, but do not prove, an exact-zero rate of `2^-32`.
Under that model, three retained roots give a mean of about `2^45.4` swept
values, or about 15 A100-hours at the recorded production rate. Two computed
witnesses were found in successful runs lasting 2.45 and 16.16 GPU-hours. All
six recorded production runs total about 71.7 GPU-hours.

### Historical companion

[`paper_r20_barrier.pdf`](paper_r20_barrier.pdf) records finite negative
experiments on the original construction. It predates the context-shaped
construction and is retained as research history. It is not an impossibility
or lower-bound result, and its headline performance discussion is superseded
by `paper_submask_r20.pdf`.

The September 2026 ePrint submissions 111557 and 111558 were declined under
the archive's general editorial criteria without a paper-specific technical
error being identified. The PDFs currently in this repository are revised
manuscripts and have not been assigned ePrint report numbers.

## Exact Setting

The demonstrated object is the one-block reduced-round SHA-256 compression
function:

- the standard SHA-256 initial value is fixed;
- feed-forward is retained;
- all 256 target output bits are fixed;
- the input is one unrestricted 512-bit block (`W[0]` through `W[15]`);
- only the target digest is supplied to the construction.

This differs from padded SHA-256, a free-start or pseudo-preimage setting, a
partial-output problem, and a collision. No result here applies to full
64-round SHA-256. Six R19 targets and one R20 target were generated by hashing
random messages, so their existence was known when the experiments began. The
all-ones target is a fixed benchmark. These examples do not prove that every
256-bit target is attainable or that a modeled mean is target-independent.

## Construction Utilities

The disk table is a NumPy array of `2^32` signed 32-bit entries (16 GiB):

```bash
python3 code/build_sigma0_table.py --out sigma0_u_table.npy
python3 code/build_sigma0_table.py --check sigma0_u_table.npy
```

The table stores one representative `u` for each attained value of
`sigma0(u) - u`. Its exact image contains 2,721,603,628 values. The `-1`
sentinel hides the single legitimate representative `u = 0xFFFFFFFF`; this
changes at most one table entry and is documented in the builder.

Table-free algebra checks for the newer family:

```bash
python3 code/submask_family.py
```

The original GPU implementation and the newer fused implementation are:

```bash
python3 code/h100_extended.py --help
python3 code/submask_triton.py --verify
python3 code/submask_triton.py --bench
```

The recorded R20 production configuration used three 16-GiB root tables on
80-GiB GPUs. Running a new production campaign is not required to verify any
published witness or to rebuild the papers.

## Padding and Other Exploratory Work

No padded SHA-256 preimage is claimed. `code/padded_preimage.py` records an
exploratory closed-form way to impose the three one-block padding words, but
the archived 167-context experiment produced no final witness and was too
small to support a negative conclusion.

The repository also retains schedule-difference and near-collision
experiments. Their observed minima are compatible with chance and are not
claimed as cryptanalytic results. They are separated from the evidence used
for the two manuscripts.
